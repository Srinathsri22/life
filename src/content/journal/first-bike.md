I had been saving for it longer than I told anyone. The showroom smelled of rubber
and floor polish, and the salesman kept calling me "sir" in a way that made me feel
like I was getting away with something.

The paperwork took two hours. The ride home took forty minutes and I remember
almost none of it — only that I took the long way, and that I kept checking the
mirrors to see if the bike was still there behind me.
